MBFU (Me Before You): Erin Min’s First Exhibition

Year: Summer 2025

Medium: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign

MBFU (short for Me Before U) began with a poster that established the project’s visual identity, using pink tones, illustrated space of bubbles, and interconnected lines to symbolize the weaving of personal and collective stories. This visual language extended into the exhibition, where the poster and accompanying book defined the thematic system and transformed the project into a memorable monument.

Credit: Erin Min — Designer (visual language/identity, poster, book); oxxy444 — Exhibition Support, Editing, Marketing; Roki Kim — Managing, Photography; Daniel Lee — Installation Assistance; Pancommunications — Production

Things you see when you slow down

Year: Fall 2025

Medium: Adobe InDesign, Perfect Binder, Coptic, Coding

Dimensions: 2.5’ x 7’

Credit: Erin Min — Concept, Design, Production, Binding, Printing, Coding

Web Experience

Slow Language / 느림의 언어 is a publication and digital experience that examines how language and design shape our sense of time. Through a hand-bound scroll and a paired interactive webpage, reading becomes a slow, embodied act defined by rhythm, pause, and attention. By moving between the stillness of paper and the gentle motion of the screen, the project frames language as a temporal landscape—inviting readers to notice silence, breath, and the act of slowing down itself.

Thank you for always doing your Best

Year: Fall 2023

Medium: Linoleum, Newspaper, Paper, Printing Ink

Dimensions: 12.8"x12.8"

Credit: Erin Min — Concept, Design, Painting, Drawing, Marketing, Planning

The phrase carved on the stamp is a phrase used on a Korean traditional stamp people use a lot at school. The phrase directly translates to "Good Job!". It is an iconic stamp that has good feelings resonating with it. It is also a childhood memory for a lot of Korean children, including me. I created my own variation on the stamp. There are usually a boy and a girl, but instead, I carved two women to represent my color and art style.
